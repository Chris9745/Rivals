The history of the RIVALS

RIVALS is built around short, high-intensity FPS duels. Players challenge others by stepping on duel pads and compete in matches ranging from 1v1 to 5v5, with victory going to the first side to reach five round wins. The game emphasizes quick engagements, aim skill, and tactical positioning across multiple maps and modes.
